Recap

The crisis had a significant impact on Abu Dhabi and all other regional destinations.

Awareness rose as Abu Dhabi and Dubai hit the headlines. This ‘toxic’ lift is a chance to reframe the narrative for people who just learned about us, while desire to visit in 3 years (consideration) and 12 months (intent) fell, and ‘will never visit’ rose for both.
